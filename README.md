# Bellbuoy TV

An accessible TV and radio player for Windows, built for screen reader users. Made by Brice Mull (Rockoutloud11).

Bellbuoy TV puts your TV, movies, music and radio in plain lists you can move through with the arrow keys, and plays them in a player you control from the keyboard. It works with NVDA, JAWS and Narrator.

## What it plays

- **Your Plex server**, plus Plex's free movies and Live TV.
- **Free services:** Pluto TV, Tubi, PBS, Xumo Play, The Roku Channel and YouTube.
- **Your own subscriptions:** Prime Video, Paramount+, HBO Max, discovery+, Peacock, Apple TV, Disney+ (with Hulu and ESPN) and YouTube TV (live channels, guide, recordings, and recording shows).
- **Radio:** local AM and FM stations and tens of thousands of internet stations, with song names.
- **SiriusXM**, with your own account: every channel, shows and podcasts, and live game scores.
- **Spotify**, with your own account: songs, albums, artists and playlists, Made For You, your library and podcasts; like songs and make and edit playlists.
- **Apple Music**, with your Apple Music subscription: search, For You, charts, your library and playlists.
- **YouTube Music:** songs, albums, artists and playlists, and your own library with your Google account.
- **Audio description:** each service's described titles in one place, played with description on.

## Your choice of services

Settings has a list of every service: uncheck the ones you don't use and they're left out. Put your ZIP code in Settings (where you actually are) and YouTube TV's local channels play even on wireless home internet.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/rockoutloud11/Bellbuoy/releases/latest). There are two downloads:

- **BellbuoyTV-Setup**: the installer. Run it and press Enter on each page.
- **BellbuoyTV-Portable**: a zip to unzip onto a flash drive or any folder. Nothing to install; carry it from computer to computer.

You need Windows 10 or 11.

Once you have version 0.9.111 or newer, Bellbuoy TV tells you when there's a new version and updates itself when you say so (Help, Check for updates checks any time).

## Help

The full guide is the README.txt that comes with Bellbuoy TV, also under Help, Guide, inside the program. Press F1 in the program for the keys.

## Why it exists

The apps and websites of most streaming services are hard, and sometimes impossible, to use with a screen reader, and they often get worse with each redesign. Bellbuoy TV gives people who use NVDA, JAWS and Narrator plain lists and a keyboard player for services they already pay for.
