# Bellbuoy TV

An accessible TV and radio player for Windows, built for screen reader users.

Made by Brice Mull (Rockoutloud11).

## Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/rockoutloud11/Bellbuoy/releases/latest):

- **BellbuoyTV-Setup** (.exe): the installer.
- **BellbuoyTV-Portable** (.zip): the portable copy. Unzip it onto a flash drive or any folder and run BellbuoyTV.exe, with nothing to install.

Bellbuoy TV runs on Windows 10 and 11. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

## What it does

- **Plex**: your own Plex server, and Plex's free movies and Live TV.
- **Free services**: Pluto TV, Tubi, PBS, Xumo Play and The Roku Channel.
- **YouTube**.
- **Your own subscriptions**: Prime Video, Paramount+, HBO Max, discovery+, Peacock, Apple TV and Disney+ (with Hulu and ESPN), each played in the service's own player.
- **Radio**: local AM and FM stations, stations near you, and tens of thousands of internet stations, with song names.
- **SiriusXM**, with your own account: every channel, shows and podcasts, and live game scores.
- **Audio description**, found and turned on automatically wherever a service has it.

Everything is built for the keyboard and works with NVDA, JAWS and Narrator. The full guide comes with the program: open the Help menu, then "Guide (README)". F1 lists the keys.

## About

Bellbuoy TV is an independent, free project. It is not made by, endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with any of the services it works with. Their names belong to their owners and are used only to say what Bellbuoy works with. You need your own account and subscription for each paid service. Bellbuoy plays titles in each service's own player, doesn't get around copy protection, and doesn't record or download anything. Your passwords are typed on each service's own sign-in page; Bellbuoy never sees them.
